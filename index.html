Our Philosophy

Shinrin Yoku translates to "forest bathing"—the practice of immersing oneself in nature to restore the soul. Here, we translate this ancient wisdom into a multi-sensory culinary journey.

Every element of our space and menu is designed to slow time. From the tactile grain of charred cedar tables to the delicate aroma of pine-smoked dashi, we invite you to disconnect from the digital and reconnect with the elemental.

The Concept

Culinary Mastery

Chef Kenji Sato approaches every plate as a landscape. With two decades of experience in Kyoto's most prestigious kitchens, he brings a rigorous precision to the wild, seasonal bounty of the land.

“We do not cook the ingredients; we listen to them until they tell us how they wish to be presented.”
